UABankSim 🏦
UABankSim is a deep, event-driven economic simulator that puts you in the CEO's chair of a commercial bank in Ukraine. Your objective is to build a resilient financial empire by balancing aggressive market expansion with strict regulatory compliance.

Unlike classic business simulators where you produce and sell physical goods, your main product here is money, and your primary tool is risk management.

🌟 Key Features
Deep General Ledger Architecture: Absolutely every action in the game (from issuing a loan to fueling a cash-in-transit armored vehicle) generates real accounting entries that impact your Balance Sheet and P&L.

Scalable Gameplay: Start your career managing a single department (like card products or foreign exchange) and rise to control the macroeconomics of the entire bank at the Board of Directors level.

Ukrainian Market Realities: Features integration of historical NBU (National Bank of Ukraine) data, including key policy rates and crises. Manage mandatory reserves, capital adequacy ratios (H2), NBU deposit certificates, and government bonds (OVDP).

Multi-level Infrastructure: Develop a network of 4 distinct branch types, decide between paying for outsourced card processing or building your own in-house center (CAPEX vs OPEX), and manage ATM fleets and cash logistics.

Business Segmentation: Juggle three independent business verticals: Mass Retail (high volume, high NPL), SME (cash management and payroll projects), and VIP Banking (concierge services, individual exchange rates, and extreme reputation sensitivity).

Stress Tests: Survive blackouts, bank runs, hacker attacks, and unannounced financial monitoring inspections.

🏗 Technical Stack & Architecture
The project is built on a modular, Event-Driven Architecture. The core simulation is completely isolated from individual product modules. This allows for seamless scaling and the addition of new banking mechanics without having to rewrite the foundational code.
